ABOUT US

Inspiring the next generation
of pilots and racers.

At Havoc EFS, we believe every child has the potential to achieve greatness. We make the skies and the tracks more accessible, exciting, and confidence to make their dreams take flight.

OUR MISSION

To inspire and educate young aspirants through hands-on aviation and motorsport training, building confidence, technical skills, and a genuine passion for flight and racing from an early age.

OUR VISION

To become India's leading youth aviation and motorsport education platform — where every child gets the opportunity to build, fly, race, and dream without limits.

OUR VALUES

Safety first, curiosity always. We foster a structured, inclusive, and encouraging environment where young minds grow through exploration, teamwork, and practical achievement.

LEARN

Acquire theoretical knowledge and practical understanding in aerodynamics, RC flying, and motorsport engineering.

PRACTICE

Apply your skills hands-on with state-of-the-art simulators, RC modeling, and expert-guided training sessions.

EXPERIENCE

Thrill in the real-world application of your passion through competitions, events, and actual track & flight time.
