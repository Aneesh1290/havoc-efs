Aviation, Aeromodelling, Flight Simulation & Motorsport Education for Young Minds
Havoc EFS introduces young aspirants to the exciting world of Aeromodelling, RC Aviation, Model Building, RC Flying, Flight Simulation and RC Motorsport through structured, practical and hands-on learning.
Our programs are designed to take students progressively from Beginner to Advanced Levels, helping them understand not only how models and simulators are operated, but also how RC models are designed, built, assembled, configured, maintained and safely flown or driven.
The objective is to encourage curiosity, technical understanding, practical skills, coordination and a passion for aviation and motorsport from an early age.

OUR LEARNING PHILOSOPHY – FROM BUILDING TO OPERATING
At Havoc EFS, our objective is not simply to teach a student how to fly an RC aircraft, operate a simulator or drive an RC car. We want young minds to understand how it is designed, built, assembled, configured, maintained, controlled and finally safely operated.
