RC CARS:
BUILDING, SETUP
& DRIVING

Build • Tune • Drive • Master

Recommended Age: 10+

Explore the world of RC motorsport through hands-on learning. Students learn how RC cars are assembled, configured, maintained and controlled while developing an understanding of chassis, suspension, power systems, electronics and track driving.

01 COURSE OVERVIEW

From Building the Car to Driving the Track

This program introduces students to the engineering and driving aspects of RC motorsport.

Students learn how different RC cars work, how their mechanical and electronic components fit together, how vehicle setup affects handling and performance, and how to control the vehicle in a track environment.

The program provides practical exposure to:

  • RC Car Assembly
  • Mechanical Components
  • Vehicle Setup
  • Electronics
  • Power Systems
  • Steering & Suspension
  • Transmitter Configuration
  • Maintenance
  • Troubleshooting
  • Driving Techniques
  • Track Practice
The objective is to help students understand how vehicle setup can influence handling and performance.

02 EXPLORE DIFFERENT RC CARS

Different Cars. Different Power Systems.

Students can be introduced to different categories of RC cars according to their age, experience and skill level.

⚡ Electric RC Cars

Explore battery-powered RC vehicles and their electrical power systems.

🔥 Nitro RC Cars

Understand RC cars powered by nitro-fuel engines and their associated systems.

⛽ Gas / Petrol RC Cars

Explore larger petrol-powered RC vehicles and their mechanical power systems.

🛠️ DIY & Ready Kits

Learn to assemble commercially available RC car kits and understand the components.
