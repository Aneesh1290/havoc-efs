RC CARS:
BUILDING, SETUP
& DRIVING

Build • Tune • Drive • Master

Recommended Age: 10+

Explore the world of RC motorsport through hands-on learning. Students learn how RC cars are assembled, configured, maintained and controlled while developing an understanding of chassis, suspension, power systems, electronics and track driving.

01 COURSE OVERVIEW

From Building the Car to Driving the Track

This program introduces students to the engineering and driving aspects of RC motorsport.

Students learn how different RC cars work, how their mechanical and electronic components fit together, how vehicle setup affects handling and performance, and how to control the vehicle in a track environment.

Students gain hands-on exposure to:

  • RC Car Assembly
  • Mechanical Components
  • Vehicle Setup
  • Electronics
  • Power Systems
  • Steering & Suspension
  • Transmitter Configuration
  • Maintenance
  • Troubleshooting
  • Driving Techniques
  • Track Practice
Understand how vehicle setup can influence handling and performance.
