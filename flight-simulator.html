FLIGHT SIMULATOR
COACHING & TRAINING

Explore • Simulate • Learn • Advance

Recommended Age: 10+

Progress from basic flight controls and cockpit familiarisation to general aviation, commercial airliner and advanced aircraft simulation through structured, hands-on simulator training.

01 COURSE OVERVIEW

Experience the World of Flight Simulation

Havoc EFS offers structured Flight Simulator Coaching & Training designed to introduce young aspirants to simulated flying and progressively expose them to more advanced aircraft, cockpit systems, instruments and operating procedures.

Students can develop an understanding of:

  • Aircraft Controls
  • Cockpit Systems
  • Flight Instruments
  • Aircraft Procedures
  • Navigation
  • Take-Off & Landing
  • Flight Management
  • Simulator Operations
  • Different Aircraft Configurations
The program progresses according to the student's age, experience and skill level.

02 GENERAL AVIATION SIMULATOR

Start With the Fundamentals

Students can begin with simulator configurations representing basic real-world general aviation aircraft, such as Cessna-type aircraft and similar platforms.

Cockpit Familiarisation

  • Basic Flight Controls
  • Yoke Operation
  • Throttle Controls
  • Rudder Pedals
  • Basic Aircraft Instruments

Basic Flight Operations

  • Engine-Start Concepts
  • Taxiing
  • Take-Off
  • Climb
  • Straight & Level Flight

09 SKILLS DEVELOPED

More Than Just Flying

Through simulator-based learning, students can develop:

  • Coordination: Build control coordination through repeated simulator practice.
  • Cockpit Awareness: Become familiar with layouts, instruments and controls.
  • Procedural Thinking: Develop awareness of checklists and structured procedures.
  • Navigation Awareness: Understand basic navigation and flight-planning concepts.
  • Aircraft Familiarity: Experience how different aircraft use different systems.
  • Confidence: Progressively build familiarity with simulated flight operations.
